# LALIGA Pulse – Project Progress Save
**Saved:** 2026-10-07

---

## Core Vision
LALIGA Pulse is the official real-time collective voice and emotional operating system of LALIGA fans.  
It turns every match into a high-stakes, participatory, identity-rich experience — making regular league games feel closer to a World Cup final — while creating strong reasons to open the product before, during, after matches, and on non-match days.

**One-sentence pitch:**  
LALIGA Pulse is the official collective voice of fans — a live layer where supporters shape the official narrative of every match through timed high-stakes moments, earn lasting status, and experience football with World Cup final intensity every single week.

---

## Opportunity Areas Addressed
- Primary: In-match participation + Structured community
- Secondary: Extended experience + Data & narrative amplification

---

## Key Systems
1. **Official Fan Pulse** – Curated, timed collective votes that become official narrative assets
2. **Pulse Rank / Identity** – Persistent season-long (and multi-season) status and recognition
3. **Emotional Temperature** – Live emotional climate of the fanbase as proprietary metric
4. **Match Trace** – Personal record of every decision and feeling across the season
5. **Always-On Layer** – Season arcs, echoes, knowledge progression, non-match rituals
