02 · Concept summary

One line: a fan reputation layer that runs all season.

Fans call what happens in the match — predictions, votes, the moment of the night — and earn a public reputation that follows them across matches, clubs and the season. Every call lands as a tile; the season is the mosaic they built. They compete as individuals, as club stands, and as small blocks.

The league gets three assets it cannot buy elsewhere:

  • A fan preference graph — what the stand expects, moment by moment.
  • A new broadcast metric — The Heat, argued about on air.
  • Sponsor inventory attached to genuine attention, not impressions.

In-match participation

Timed Moments and micro-predictions through the ninety.

Structured community

Club stands and blocks with their own standing and status.

Data & narrative

A proprietary Heat index that gives commentators a second storyline.

Extended experience

Missions, arcs and echoes that make Tuesday part of the season.

All four opportunity areas, one loop — not four features.

04 · Prototype scope

What is live at Demo Day — and what is deliberately conceptual.

Live, working now

  • Ritual and club identity; the fan enters the night, not a form.
  • Vote on real Moments with a live aggregate tally and one-vote-per-fan guard.
  • Predictions and points; season reputation and rank that persist across matches.
  • Global, club and block leaderboards — blocks can be created and joined by code.
  • Reveal card, EN/ES toggle, installable PWA, offline fallback.
  • A real Postgres schema (Neon free tier) with an append-only events table as the source of truth.

Conceptual at this stage

  • Official LALIGA SSO and app-tab embedding.
  • Live rights-cleared match feed (adapter contract defined).
  • Broadcast lower-third and stadium ribbon integrations.
  • Sponsor contracts and production ad-serving.
  • Full ES/EN/AR localization with RTL.
  • Production anti-fraud and anomaly detection.

We chose a narrow, honest prototype: the loop and its evidence are real; the league integrations are scoped, not pretended.

07 · Integration plan

It lives inside LALIGA — not beside it.

App

A tab in the official LALIGA app. Existing SSO — one identity, rank travels with the fan. No new account.

Broadcast

The Heat as a clean lower-third. Aggregate only; never a named fan. One line partners can take.

Stadium

The same call on the ribbon at half-time. People in the ground and at home are one stand.

Data

A feed adapter maps rights-cleared match events into the Mosaico contract. The prototype already runs on that shape with a mock feed.

Commercial

Sponsored moments and blocks, with the league desk owning the question and the result.

Rollout

One club, one matchday pilot → league-wide ES → LATAM → MENA (AR/RTL). Each step reuses the same loop.

What we need from LALIGA: feed access for one pilot club, SSO, a brand review, and a matchday to prove the loop in public.

08 · Success metrics

One north star, measured from real events.

North star: Weekly Active Predictors (WAP) per matchday — fans who make at least one call in a rolling seven-day window. It rewards recurrence, not reach.

KPIModelHow the prototype evidences it
Matchday participation rate8–15%Voters ÷ matchday reach, from one event row per vote.
Calls per fan per match4.6Vote + forecast events grouped by fan and match.
D7 return, ritual cohort55–70%Cohort of fans who completed the ritual, tracked by fan id.
Block join rate12%Join events ÷ active fans.
Second-screen attach3–6%Broadcast QR/deep-link opens reconciled to matchday reach.
Reveal cards shared18%Share events ÷ Reveal views.

Proof at Demo Day: open /board — event counts, dedupe and leaderboards are computed from the same rows these KPIs read. Nothing here is a screenshot.

Figures are modelled assumptions, clearly labelled; every prototype action writes an event, so each row becomes measurable in the pilot. A/B design: ritual-first vs value-first onboarding.

09 · Economic sustainability

Sponsorship attached to attention, not impressions.

Presented moments

One curated Moment per match carries a named partner. They never set the question or touch the result.

Block sponsorship

Clubs, bars and cities host blocks — a second, local line of partnership with measurable membership.

Season partner

A presenting partner across the season arc, leaderboards and the Reveal card.

Why the cost base is honest

Built to prove itself on free tiers: scale-to-zero Postgres, static/edge delivery, one append-only table. Infrastructure cost stays near zero until usage earns the right to spend.

"No cost until proven" is the operating model, not a slide promise.

Guardrails

  • No wagering, odds, stakes or cash-out.
  • Sponsor never influences questions or results.
  • Rank cannot be bought — protecting the product's credibility.
  • Ad load capped so the night never feels like inventory.

Revenue order: prove attention → sell the moment → renew on measured recall. No inventory is sold before the loop works.

What could go wrong — and how we close it.

Data protectionGDPR + UAE PDPL. Pseudonymous fan id, data minimisation, age gate, defined retention, aggregate-only outputs.
